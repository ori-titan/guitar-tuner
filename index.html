    Settings

    Warning: this listens to a longer slice of sound (about 170 ms instead of 85 ms). It is more precise, especially for the low strings, but uses about 4× more processing power and battery, reacts a little slower, and may stutter on older phones.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Press E A D G or B to lock the tuner to a string with that note. Press the same key again for the next string with the same note (E: low E, then high E), and once more to go back to automatic mode. This works with any keyboard language.
